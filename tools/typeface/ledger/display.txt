# MERCS Ledger Display: glyph source, drawn by hand on the pixel grid.
##
# Grid (one cell = one font pixel, 100 font units):
#   row 0-1   accents of capitals / ascender top (row 1)
#   row 2     cap height top (caps are 9 rows: 2-10)
#   row 5     x-height top (lowercase is 6 rows: 5-10)
#   row 10    the last row of ink on the baseline
#   row 11-13 descenders (3 rows)
# Block:  @ <char | U+XXXX | mark name> <top row> [adv=N] [lsb=N]
#         then the glyph's rows from <top row> down, '#' ink and '.' empty, all one width.
#         The advance is the width + 1 (one column of air) unless adv= says otherwise;
#         lsb= adds empty columns on the left.
# Compose: = <char> <base> <mark>  (mark centred over the base; marks have their own rows)
##
# Hand rules (what makes it this face, not a generic pixel font):
#   1. Chisel-cut bar ends: E F L T Z end their bars with a one-pixel tick, the way a knife
#      stops a cut; C and G open with barbs at both terminals.
#   2. Ledger strokes: l i j start with a pen flag to the left; l and t end with a foot.
#   3. S and s turn on a two-step diagonal spine, never a flat bar (s never reads as z).
#   4. Plain, open capitals and digits: the numbers a player decides on carry no decoration.
#      Zero is slashed (O and 0 never meet), 1 has a flag and a foot, I has bars, B has a
#      square back and a small upper bowl (never 8), 4 and 3 are unbroken single strokes.
#
# Display: the heading cut. Every vertical stroke is 2 px, horizontals stay 1 px
# (a broad nib's contrast); it began as the Regular thickened one column to the right,
# then M N W w % @ , ; " “ ” * … ° and the diaeresis and tilde were redrawn by hand. Use it at 3x (42 px at 1080p) or larger.
