MERCS Ledger (Regular, Display)

An original pixel typeface drawn for MERCS (2026) by the project's art agent, after the
hand-ruled company ledgers and carved tallies of a mercenary band: plain open letters with
chisel-cut bar ends and pen flags. Drawn cell by cell as text bitmaps in
tools/typeface/ledger/ and built with tools/typeface/build_font.py. Not derived from any
existing font. Owned by the project.
