# hub.json: the hub's contract screens

Read by `presentation/world/terms_panel.gd` (Phase 5 C8). Presentation only: what the employer
accepts is `economy.json` "terms".

- `terms.price_step_pct`: one click on Ask more or Ask less moves the asked price by this percent of the posted pay (at least one coin).
- `terms.advance_step_pct`: one click on More now or Less now moves the advance by this percent of the asked price (at least one coin), between none and the whole price.
